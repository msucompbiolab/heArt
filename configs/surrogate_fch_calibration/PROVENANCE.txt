RV passive-stiffness reconciliation (operator decision, 2026-06-15)
------------------------------------------------------------------
material_params.json carries Cparam_rv = 380 Pa, a SAMPLING-convention value (the
material DoE explored RV stiffness ~325-400). The four-chamber OPERATING convention
deliberately uses the SOFT Cparam_rv = 40 Pa (canonical FCH_BASE_PASSIVE; thin
near-indeterminate RV free wall, unloaded-reference convention) -- the stiff sampled
RV will not de-load (the unload diverges on the RV ramp). The forward run therefore
uses the OPERATING Cparam_rv = 40 Pa for both the unload and the loading
(fch_baseline_config rv_cparam_operating=True, the default; job_fch_closedloop.sh
passes it to the unload). The other 7 locked dims (Tmax_{lv,rv,la,ra}, Cparam_{lv,la,ra})
are used EXACTLY. Override with --sampled-rv-cparam to use the locked 380 Pa.
